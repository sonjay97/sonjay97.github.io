With AI being the talk of the town and apparently the demise of all engineers + somehow the savior of all mankind, I decided to make an AI tool for my team. Or, my manager told me that I have to make an AI tool for my team to signal to the rest of our company that we are #Agentic. Wunderbar.

Ok but what LLm tool is actually useful for my team

Well, LLMs are only really that great when they are grounded. What does that mean? well we are of course describing humbleness. When my AI chatbot starts acting like it knows everything, well, it becomes insufferable to work with. Just like a few ex-coworkers I remember... .

Ok but how do I make AI humble and grounded

Now we get into the fun part. Graph RAG. I am using Neo4j and good ole RAG to make a llm chat bot thingie that only gets information based on what my team has agreed upon is actually useful. Ok so now all we did is build a search tool, cause we basically just ask the llm to search for information that we have pre populated in a db. and then if the LLM wants, it can add its cutesy little "context" aware chat stuff.
